What to look at

  • Proof, not a repeat. The visitor sees the recruiter's own words, the team and the responsibilities. The feed card showed only a summary.
  • What's withheld is the thing you sell. The black bars cover the CFO's name and the apply email. The recruiter's name is blurred and the post link is gone.
  • Cut-off where it matters. The text fades out before the rest of the post, so it reads as real but not complete.
  • Mobile order. Today the role card sits below the price card on phones. For title and card visitors I'd put the snapshot right under the headline, as shown.
  • One button, same promise. "Unlock Now" under the post goes to the same checkout as the card.
